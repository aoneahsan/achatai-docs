Getting Started

  • Installation & access

    Use AChat in your browser or get the Android app from Google Play. Anonymous rooms need no account; personal chats need a Google sign-in and a username.

  • Quick Start

    Start your first AChat conversation, with a friend by username or in an anonymous room by link, and save your recovery key.

Features

  • Personal chats & contacts

    Add people by username, invite link or QR code, handle message requests and blocks, and choose who sees when you're online and whether you send read receipts.

  • Devices & recovery key

    Link another device to your AChat account, compare security codes, remove a lost device, and use your recovery key to bring your history back.

  • Anonymous rooms

    How AChat's anonymous rooms work without an account — room IDs, display names, passwords, how long messages are kept, and what anonymous doesn't mean.

  • Passwords & encryption

    Which AChat chats are end-to-end encrypted, what a room password protects, and the limits — open rooms, communities and status aren't end-to-end encrypted.

  • Groups & communities

    Private encrypted groups for people who know each other, and communities with channels, threads, roles, reports and bans. Which ones need an account.

  • Status

    Post a status for your contacts that disappears after 24 hours, choose who sees it, and know that status updates aren't end-to-end encrypted.

  • Kept rooms & your account

    Keep an anonymous AChat room past its default history, release it, and manage your account — username, data export and account deletion.

  • Threads & replies

    Reply to a message, or reply in a thread so a side conversation stays in one place. Threads work in chats, groups, rooms and community channels.

  • Files, voice notes & media

    Share files of any type, photos, videos and voice notes in AChat, within your plan's file allowances, and see where media from a chat is collected.

  • Manage messages

    Edit, delete, clear, forward, pin and restore AChat messages; per-chat Trash; disappearing messages and view once; polls; archiving chats, messages and replies; saving a copy of a chat.

  • Writing messages

    Drafts, spoilers, maths, mentions, stickers, dictation, on-device translation and read aloud in AChat, and which of them leave your device.

  • Search & offline history

    AChat search runs on your device over the messages it holds. Chats you've opened stay readable offline, and messages you write offline wait in an outbox.

  • Embeddable widget

    Put an AChat anonymous room or community channel on your website, in the page or as a chat button, and choose which websites may show it.

  • Notifications & email

    AChat notifications are opt-in and never include the message text. Which emails AChat sends to your Google email address, and when.

  • Appearance, language & shortcuts

    One Appearance panel for theme, colour, text size and motion; five interface languages including Arabic; keyboard shortcuts and focus mode in AChat.

  • Privacy on this device

    AChat settings that protect a shared or borrowed device — privacy screen, neutral tab title, clipboard auto-clear and Wipe from this device.

  • Device & location history

    Some AChat chats record device and location history. What's recorded, who can see it, how long it's kept, and how a chat asks before you enter.

  • Plans & pricing

    AChat's Free, Pro and Team / Family plans, the four allowances they change, how paying by card or bank transfer works, and how referrals earn plan months.

  • Page tours and How AChat works

    The page tour on Chats, Features, Plans and pricing, Communities and Account, and the six-step How AChat works page.

  • Speak, Draft help and Catch me up

    Speak turns a recording into text in your message box, Draft help rewrites, shortens or translates your own draft, and Catch me up summarises an open room for you alone.
