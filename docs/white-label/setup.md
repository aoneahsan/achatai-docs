# Setting up your copy

**After you pay, the AChat team does the setup and writes to you about each part.** There's nothing to install on your side.

1. **The licence email.** Your licence key and certificate arrive at the email address you bought with. Keep both: renewals ask for the key.
2. **Your domain.** Tell the AChat team the address your copy should run at, such as `chat.yourname.com`. The domain stays yours, so if connecting it needs a DNS record on your side, the team sends you the exact one to add.
3. **Your name, logo and colours.** Send them, and they're applied to every screen.
4. **Live.** Once your copy runs at your domain, it serves its licence file and anyone can [check it](/white-label/verify-a-licence).

The payment page belongs to Polar, which takes the payment; the licence is issued once Polar confirms it, never from the page you return to.
