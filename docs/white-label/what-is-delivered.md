# What you get per platform

**A licence covers the platforms you pay for, and AChat ships two today.** An extension, desktop app or package isn't offered, because AChat doesn't ship one.

## Web app

AChat in the browser at your own address: personal chats, groups, communities, status and anonymous rooms, with your name on every screen. It's the same app that runs at achat.aoneahsan.com, kept apart from it: the people who use your copy don't get AChat accounts, and their chats never mix with AChat's.

## Mobile app (Android and iOS)

The Android app under your name, published from your own store account. The iOS build is made from the same app when you provide or fund an Apple developer account. Store developer accounts are a cost you choose, so they're yours.

## What's the same in every copy

Encryption works as it does in AChat. Personal chats, private groups and rooms with a password are end-to-end encrypted; communities, open rooms and status aren't. See [Security & encryption model](/concepts/security-and-encryption) for what that does and doesn't cover.

Your copy carries your name, so the people who use it read your privacy policy and terms, not AChat's.

## What isn't included

Changes made only for you. They're [custom work](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work), quoted and paid for separately.
