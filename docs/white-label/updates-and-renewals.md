# Updates and renewals

**Every licence and buyout includes six months of AChat updates from the day you pay.** An update is a fix or improvement the AChat team makes to AChat itself, brought to your copy. Changes made only for you are custom work.

## Renewing

A renewal adds three, six or twelve months. It starts at your current end date, or today if that date has already passed. Renew on the [updates page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) with your licence key and the email address the licence was issued to; the prices are listed there.

## Without a renewal

Your copy keeps running on the last version it got. Nothing is switched off.
