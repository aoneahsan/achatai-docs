# White-label questions

## Do I get the source code with a licence?

No. A licence doesn't disclose or transfer the source code. The code changes hands only with a [buyout](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## Is my licence exclusive?

No. Until someone buys AChat outright, the AChat team may license it to other buyers as well.

## What happens after someone buys AChat outright?

AChat comes off sale and nobody else can license it. Licences sold before the buyout keep their terms, and they can still be checked.

## Can you change AChat just for us?

Yes, as custom work: changes made only for one buyer are quoted and paid for separately. Ask on the [custom work page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## When do we get iOS?

When you provide or fund an Apple developer account. The iOS build is made from the same app as Android.

## Who pays for the domain and store accounts?

You do. Third-party costs you choose, such as your domain, store developer accounts or paid services, are yours.

## Can a licence be revoked?

Yes. A licence used outside its terms, or on a domain it doesn't cover, may be revoked.
