# Moving from Threema to AChat

**A messenger that asks for no phone number or email, with separate backup options for your ID and your chats.** Choose Threema if you want to give no personal data at all when you sign up. Choose AChat if you also want rooms anyone can join by link, and a chat you can put on a website.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [AChat and Threema](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/threema).

## How to move across

1. [Sign in to AChat](/getting-started/quick-start) and pick a username. It asks for no phone number.
2. Tell the people you talk to on Threema your username, or send them your link or QR code ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
3. Save your recovery key somewhere only you can reach ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). It's what brings your history back if you lose every device.
4. Keep Threema open until the people you need have moved. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.

## How they differ

| Feature | AChat | Threema |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://threema.ch/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Partly (<a href="https://threema.ch/en/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

AChat has no voice or video calls. Your encrypted history comes back only with another linked device or your recovery key. Lose both, and nobody can restore it, including us.
