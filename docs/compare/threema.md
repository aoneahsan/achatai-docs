# Moving from Threema to AChat

**Threema asks for no phone number or email, and backs up your ID and your chats separately.** If giving no personal data at sign-up is the reason you chose it, keep it. An AChat account signs in with Google, so it isn't as bare. What AChat adds is rooms anyone can join by link, and a chat you can put on a website.

Checked 2026-11-07. See [AChat and Threema](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/threema) for the side-by-side.

## Two ways in

Decide first how much you want to give AChat, because it changes where you start.

**Nothing at all.** Choose **Continue anonymously** and create a room ([anonymous rooms](/features/anonymous-chats)). You pick a display name for that room and share its link. Give it a password if only certain people should read it.

**An account.** [Sign in with Google](/getting-started/quick-start) and choose a username, which is how people find you, much like a Threema ID. Then:

1. Save your recovery key. Threema keeps an ID backup; on AChat, the recovery key or another linked device is what brings your history back ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)).
2. Send your Threema contacts your username or invite link ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
3. If you run a website, build a room for it on the [widget page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/widget).

Your Threema chats don't come across. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.
