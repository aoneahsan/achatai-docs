# Moving from SimpleX Chat to AChat

**SimpleX Chat has no user identifiers at all, not even random ones.** If that's the point for you, nothing here beats it. AChat goes the other way on purpose: people find you by a username. On top of that come anonymous rooms you can share by link or put on a website.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison: [AChat and SimpleX Chat](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/simplex-chat).

## Picking a name you're happy to be found by

On SimpleX, every contact starts from a one-off link. On AChat, your username is the thing you hand out, so give it a moment's thought.

1. [Sign in](/getting-started/quick-start) and choose a username of 3 to 24 letters, numbers, dots or underscores. You can change it any time. Once you do, the old one is free for someone else.
2. Hand it out the way you're used to: as an invite link or a QR code ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)). Messages from people you haven't accepted wait in **Requests**.
3. Save the recovery key ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). With no linked device left, it's the only way your history comes back.
4. For a conversation that shouldn't touch your username, use an [anonymous room](/features/anonymous-chats) instead. The [widget page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/widget) puts one on your site.

Chats stay in SimpleX. AChat doesn't import from other apps.

## As stated

|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://simplex.chat/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://simplex.chat/faq/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

On AChat, history returns only through a linked device or your recovery key, and nobody can restore it without them, the AChat team included. AChat has no voice or video calls.
