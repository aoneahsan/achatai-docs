# Moving from WhatsApp to AChat

**WhatsApp is tied to your phone number, and it backs your chats up through Google or its own cloud storage.** If everyone you talk to is already there and you call them as often as you text, stay. AChat is for the people you'd rather reach by a username, a link or a QR code, with no phone number anywhere.

Facts below were checked on 2026-11-07. For prices and features side by side, see [AChat and WhatsApp](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/whatsapp).

## Bringing people over

On WhatsApp, someone who has your number can find you. Nobody can find you on AChat until you give them your username, so the move happens one person at a time.

1. [Sign in to AChat](/getting-started/quick-start) with Google and choose a username. You won't be asked for a phone number.
2. Copy your invite link from **Contacts** and paste it into the WhatsApp chats of the people you want to bring. Anyone who opens it can send you a contact request ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
3. Save your recovery key the moment AChat shows it ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). It takes the place of the WhatsApp backup: with no linked device left, it's the only way your history comes back.
4. Leave your WhatsApp history where it is. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps, so old messages stay readable only in WhatsApp.
5. Keep WhatsApp for calls. AChat has no voice or video calling.

## What WhatsApp's pages say

| Feature | AChat | WhatsApp |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | No (<a href="https://www.whatsapp.com/security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Partly (<a href="https://faq.whatsapp.com/481135090640375" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

"Partly" on our side means this: another linked device or your recovery key brings your encrypted history back. Lose both, and nobody can restore it, including us.
