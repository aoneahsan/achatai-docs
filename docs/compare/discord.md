# Moving from Discord to AChat

**Community chat, with Nitro as its paid upgrade for things like bigger uploads.** Choose Discord if you run a large community and want voice channels. Choose AChat if your community should welcome visitors with no account, or you want personal chats encrypted end to end.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [AChat and Discord](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/discord).

## How to move across

1. [Sign in to AChat](/getting-started/quick-start) and pick a username. It asks for no phone number.
2. Tell the people you talk to on Discord your username, or send them your link or QR code ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
3. Save your recovery key somewhere only you can reach ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). It's what brings your history back if you lose every device.
4. Keep Discord open until the people you need have moved. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.

## How they differ

| Feature | AChat | Discord |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Not stated on the pages we checked |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Not stated on the pages we checked |

AChat has no voice or video calls. Your encrypted history comes back only with another linked device or your recovery key. Lose both, and nobody can restore it, including us.
