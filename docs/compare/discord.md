# Moving from Discord to AChat

**Discord is community chat, with Nitro as the paid upgrade for things like bigger uploads.** A large community that lives in voice channels belongs on Discord; AChat has no voice. AChat suits a community that should let visitors in without an account, or people who want their one-on-one chats encrypted end to end.

Last checked 2026-11-07. Side by side: [AChat and Discord](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/discord).

## Server to community

A Discord server maps onto an AChat community fairly directly: a community holds channels, and channels hold threads.

1. [Sign in](/getting-started/quick-start) and create a community from **Communities** ([groups and communities](/features/groups-and-communities)). Pick its link ID, 8 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.
2. Choose its access. **Anonymous allowed** lets visitors join under a display name with no account. **Account needed** keeps it to people who've signed in.
3. Recreate the text channels you actually use. Make a channel private when only some members should read it.
4. Name your admins. They create channels and handle reports and bans; only the owner chooses who's an admin.
5. Post the community's link in your Discord announcements, and give the move some time.
6. Optional: put a display-name channel on your website from the [widget page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/widget).

Communities aren't end-to-end encrypted. Personal chats ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)) and private groups are, and their history returns only through a linked device or your recovery key ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). Discord history doesn't import.
