# Moving from Intercom to AChat

**Intercom is customer service software priced per seat, with its AI agent Fin charged per outcome.** A support team that wants AI answering first should stay on Intercom. AChat replaces none of that. What it offers is a room on a page, for when you never needed a support desk.

Checked 2026-11-07. See [AChat and Intercom](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/intercom) for the comparison.

## Where a room is enough

The honest test is whether anyone on your team reads the Intercom conversations. If they do, keep paying for the seats.

1. List the pages where Intercom appears. Mark the ones where visitors mostly talk to each other, or where nobody on your team replies.
2. For those, build a room or an open channel on the [widget page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/widget) ([embeddable widget](/features/embeddable-widget)).
3. Choose **Chat button** if you want it to open from a corner of the page.
4. Paste the code, and leave Intercom where your team works.
5. Have someone with an account keep the room so you can limit which websites show it.

## What Intercom's pages didn't say

We checked Intercom's pages for a free plan with no end date, a ready-made chat to add to a page, and running it on your own servers. They didn't state any of the three, so there's no feature table. The <a href="https://www.intercom.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Intercom website</a> may answer them.

On our side, AChat has a free plan, a ready-made widget, and no self-hosting. The widget isn't an agent inbox, and anyone on the page can read the room unless it has a password.
