# Moving from Crisp to AChat

**Crisp is a shared inbox for customer messages, priced per workspace.** If a team answers customers from one inbox, that's Crisp's job, and AChat doesn't do it. AChat gives you an open room on your page, with no seats to count.

Checked 2026-11-07. Compared side by side on [AChat and Crisp](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/crisp).

## When the inbox was more than you needed

AChat fits only if your Crisp conversations turn out to be visitors wanting to talk to each other, not to support.

1. Look at who actually writes in your Crisp inbox. If most messages need a reply from your team, stop here.
2. If not, build a room on the [widget page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/widget). An open channel from a community marked **Anonymous allowed** works too ([embeddable widget](/features/embeddable-widget)).
3. Switch on **Offer sign-in** if regulars should be able to keep the room. Chatting never needs it.
4. Paste the code where the Crisp widget was, or next to it.
5. Once someone keeps the room, they can list the websites allowed to show it. Until then, any site can.

## What Crisp's pages say

Crisp's pricing page confirms a free plan with no end date. The pages we checked didn't say whether Crisp offers a ready-made chat to add to a page or a version to run on your own servers, so those rows are left out.

| Feature | AChat | Crisp |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan with no end date | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

AChat is hosted only, so you can't run it on your own servers. Anyone visiting the page can read the room unless it has a password.
