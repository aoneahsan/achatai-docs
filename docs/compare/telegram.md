# Moving from Telegram to AChat

**A cloud messenger that signs in with your phone number and syncs your chats across devices.** Choose Telegram if you want your history simply there on every device, and you're fine with only secret chats adding end-to-end encryption. Choose AChat if you want personal chats and private groups encrypted end to end as standard, with no phone number.

Last checked 2026-11-07. The full comparison is on [AChat and Telegram](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/telegram).

## How to move across

1. [Sign in to AChat](/getting-started/quick-start) and pick a username. It asks for no phone number.
2. Tell the people you talk to on Telegram your username, or send them your link or QR code ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
3. Save your recovery key somewhere only you can reach ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). It's what brings your history back if you lose every device.
4. Keep Telegram open until the people you need have moved. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.

## How they differ

| Feature | AChat | Telegram |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | No (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Yes (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

AChat has no voice or video calls. Your encrypted history comes back only with another linked device or your recovery key. Lose both, and nobody can restore it, including us.
