# Moving from Telegram to AChat

**Telegram is a cloud messenger: you sign in with your phone number, and your chats are simply there on every device.** End-to-end encryption comes only with secret chats. If having everything everywhere matters more to you than that, Telegram fits. AChat encrypts personal chats and private groups end to end as standard and never asks for a phone number. The cost is that your history follows your devices, not a cloud.

Checked 2026-11-07. The side-by-side page is [AChat and Telegram](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/telegram).

## Before your first message

The habit to unlearn is "log in anywhere and it's all there". Set up your devices on day one, while you still have one signed in.

1. [Sign in to AChat](/getting-started/quick-start) on the device you use most, and choose a username. Telegram people are used to usernames, so yours works the same way when you share it.
2. Link your other phone, tablet or computer from **Account**, then **Devices** ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). The new device scans a code from the one you're signed in on.
3. Save the recovery key somewhere off those devices. It's what restores your history if every linked device is gone.
4. Add your Telegram contacts by username ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
5. Recreate the groups you care about from **Groups**, inviting people once they're your contacts. A public Telegram channel is closer to an AChat [community](/features/groups-and-communities), which isn't end-to-end encrypted.

Your Telegram history stays in Telegram. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.

| Feature | AChat | Telegram |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | No (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Partly (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Yes (<a href="https://telegram.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

That "Partly" is the trade. Another linked device or your recovery key brings your history back. Without either, nobody can restore it, the AChat team included. AChat also has no voice or video calls.
