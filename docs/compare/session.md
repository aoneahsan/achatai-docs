# Moving from Session to AChat

**Session creates your account with no identifier tied to you, sends messages through onion routing, and restores your Account ID on a new device from a recovery password.** For conversations where that matters, stay on Session. An AChat account signs in with Google. The reasons to add AChat are communities with channels and threads, and rooms people join from a link.

Checked 2026-11-07. Side by side: [AChat and Session](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/session).

## What to bring, and what to leave

A sensible split: move a group's open talk, and keep private conversations where they are.

1. Start with a room, which needs no account ([quick start](/getting-started/quick-start)). **Continue anonymously**, create one, and share its link ([anonymous rooms](/features/anonymous-chats)).
2. If the group needs somewhere to last, someone with an account creates a [community](/features/groups-and-communities) and sets it to **Anonymous allowed**, so members can still join under a display name. Communities aren't end-to-end encrypted, so they suit open talk.
3. If you do make an account, you'll get a recovery key. It does the job Session's recovery password does for your ID, but here it restores your message history ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). Save it off the device.
4. Add the people you want to keep talking to one on one by username ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).

Nothing imports from Session.

## Feature by feature

| Feature | AChat | Session |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://getsession.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| End-to-end encryption on personal chats | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://getsession.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |
| History comes back on a new device | Partly | Partly (<a href="https://getsession.org/faq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

Lose the recovery key and every linked device, and your AChat history can't be restored by anyone, including us. AChat has no voice or video calls.

We found no price to list for Session. AChat's are on the [pricing page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/pricing). Check it yourself on the <a href="https://getsession.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">Session website</a>.
