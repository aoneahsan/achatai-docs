# Moving from Element to AChat

**Element sells chat to organisations: a free Community edition, plus Enterprise and Sovereign options priced by seat or by deployment.** If you're running chat for an organisation and want your own deployment, this page isn't your move; AChat can't be run on your own servers. It's for the other case, where you used Element to keep up with friends and family and would rather start in a browser with nothing to set up.

Checked 2026-11-07. Compared in full on [AChat and Element](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/compare/element).

## Moving a personal circle

1. Open [achat.aoneahsan.com](https://achat.aoneahsan.com) in any current browser and [sign in](/getting-started/quick-start). There's nothing to set up. Choose a username; no phone number is asked for.
2. Save your recovery key ([devices and recovery](/features/devices-and-recovery)). It's shown once.
3. Add the handful of people you talk to most, by username, invite link or QR code ([personal chats and contacts](/features/personal-chats-and-contacts)).
4. Turn each small Element room into an AChat [group](/features/groups-and-communities). Groups are end-to-end encrypted. Someone who joins by link waits for an admin to approve them.
5. A larger, open room fits better as a community with channels. Communities aren't end-to-end encrypted.

Element's message history stays with Element. AChat doesn't import chats from other apps.

## What Element's pricing page says

We read Element's pricing page. It says you can sign up without a phone number. It said nothing either way about end-to-end encryption on personal chats or getting history back on a new device, so we've left those rows out.

| Feature | AChat | Element |
|---|---|---|
| Sign up without a phone number | Yes | Yes (<a href="https://element.io/en/pricing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">their page</a>) |

On AChat, personal chats are encrypted end to end. A linked device or your recovery key brings your history back, and without them nobody can restore it, including us. There are no voice or video calls.
