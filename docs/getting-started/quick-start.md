# Quick Start

**Pick someone you already talk to and send a hello. That's the whole first step.** Chats is where AChat opens, and every conversation starts there.

## Chat with someone you know

1. Open [achat.aoneahsan.com](https://achat.aoneahsan.com), choose **Use an account**, then **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose a username. It's how friends find you.
3. Save your **recovery key** when AChat shows it. It's the only way back to your history if you lose every device, and AChat shows it only once. See [Devices & recovery key](/features/devices-and-recovery).
4. Open **Contacts** and add someone by username, invite link or QR code.
5. Once they accept, open the chat and send a message.

The label under your message says **Sending**, then **Sent** once it has arrived.

## Chat without an account

1. Choose **Continue anonymously**, then **Create room**, or **Join room** with a link or ID someone sent you.
2. Pick a display name, 1–24 characters. It's the one thing others in the room see, and rooms never show when you're online or last seen.
3. Share the room's link. Anyone with it can join, unless the room has a password.

A room's messages are kept for a set number of days after each one is sent, 10 by default. Someone with an account can [keep the room](/features/keep-chats-and-accounts) longer.

## When the connection drops

Chats you've opened stay readable, and new messages wait with **Waiting to send** until you're back.
