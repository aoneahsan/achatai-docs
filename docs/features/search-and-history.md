# Search & offline history

**Search runs on your device, over the messages it holds, so your search terms stay there.**

## Search

- Open **Search** to look across your chats. Results are grouped by chat.
- It covers messages already on this device. Trash and chats that are closed for now aren't searched.
- **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K**) finds a chat or a page.

## Offline

- Chats and files you've opened are kept on the device, so they load faster and can open offline.
- Messages you write offline wait in the **Outbox** with **Waiting to send**, then send in order when a connection returns. A message says **Sent** only once it has arrived.
- If some messages can't send, you can retry them or discard any you no longer need.
- A chat with device and location history on needs a connection to open.

## Saved on this device

**Account**, then **Storage**, shows what's saved on the device. **Clear saved copies** removes saved chats and files; they download again when you open them online, and your outbox isn't touched.

Signing out removes your chats from the device and any messages still waiting to send. See [Devices & recovery key](/features/devices-and-recovery).
