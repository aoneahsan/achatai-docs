# Plans & pricing

**Paying raises allowances, not features.** Free has no end date and covers personal chats, groups, communities and anonymous rooms. Current prices and allowances are on the [pricing page](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/pricing).

## The plans

| Plan | For |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Everyday chats with the people you keep up with. No payment needed |
| **Pro** | One person, higher allowances |
| **Team / Family** | Shared seats for a family or a team. Each member's chats stay their own |

Pro and Team / Family raise four allowances: file size, files per chat, how many rooms you can keep, and how long you can keep them. The AChat team sets each plan's allowances.

## Paying

1. Choose a plan on the pricing page, on the web.
2. Transfer the amount by bank, with the reference the checkout shows in the payment details.
3. Send your payment request. The AChat team matches each payment to its reference by hand, and your plan shows as waiting until it's confirmed.

- **Cancel** from the pricing page or **Account**: your plan runs until the end of the period you paid for, and no renewal payment is expected. Part of a period isn't refunded.
- **When a paid plan ends**, you move back to Free. Nothing is deleted, everything stays readable and you keep messaging. Until you're back under Free's limits, new files in a chat that's over its limit, and new kept rooms, are refused.
- Deleting your account ends a paid plan straight away, with no refund for the time left.

## Team / Family

- One person, the **payer**, pays for a minimum number of seats and invites the others by username. Set it up as a Team or a Family; both get the same plan and allowances.
- Paying doesn't let anyone read other members' messages, chats or location. The payer manages seats and renewal, and that's all.
- You can belong to one Team / Family group at a time.
- The payer can make another member the payer, or end the group. Everyone keeps the plan until the paid period ends, then goes back to their own plan, and nothing is deleted.

## Referrals

Share your invite link. When someone creates an AChat account from it and their email is verified, you get points and a badge. Confirmed referrals also add plan months, up to a limit in any 12 months; points and badges keep counting after that. Only new accounts count. If you already have that plan or higher, an earned month starts when your current plan ends.
