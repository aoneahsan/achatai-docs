# Page tours and How AChat works

**Two ways to learn AChat without leaving it: a short tour of the page you're on, and a six-step page about the whole app.**

## The page tour

**Take a tour of this page** is the signpost button in the header, just before Appearance. It is on Chats, Features,
Plans and pricing, Communities and Account. On a phone it sits in the same top bar; on Account and Communities it sits at
the end of the page heading.

The first time you open one of those pages, a short note points at the button: *New here? Take a quick tour of this
page.* It shows once per page, and once is counted for your account, so a second device doesn't show it again. It goes
by itself after a few seconds.

The tour highlights one part of the page at a time and says what it does.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Next**, **Back** | Move one step; the arrow keys do the same |
| **All steps** | Lists every step, so you can jump to any of them |
| **Skip tour**, the close button, Esc | End the tour at once |

On a phone the step opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen, or from the top when the highlighted part sits low.
A tour link with `?tour=3` at the end opens step 3.

## How AChat works

[How AChat works](https://achat.aoneahsan.com/how-achat-works) is six steps, each with the screen where it happens:
chats with the people you know, rooms without an account, groups and communities, status, what happens when the
connection drops, and the plans. Move with **Next** and **Back**, the arrow keys, or a swipe on a phone; `?step=3` opens
step 3.

You can open it from the **Help** column at the bottom of the public pages, the sitemap, the top of **Account**, and the
Chats screen before you sign in.
