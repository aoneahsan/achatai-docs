# Privacy on this device

**These settings apply to this device only, signed in or not. They don't change anything for the people you chat with.**

## Settings

| Setting | What it does | Its limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy screen** | Blurs AChat when you switch to another tab or app, and after 90 seconds without activity | Press any key or tap to show chats again |
| **Neutral tab title** | While you're in another tab, this tab is called "New tab" and shows a plain icon | Your browser history still shows AChat |
| **Clear copied links and keys** | 45 seconds after you copy an invite link, a room link or your recovery key, it's cleared from the clipboard | Some keyboards and systems keep their own clipboard history, which this can't reach |

You can also hide the screen at once with a keyboard shortcut.

## Wipe from this device

From a chat's menu, **Wipe from this device** removes that chat's saved items from this device, then closes it:

- your unsent draft
- the room password saved on this device
- your password check for its location history
- translations shown on this device
- its entries in search on this device

It doesn't delete the chat or its messages for anyone. Other devices and people keep their copies, and opening the chat loads its messages again.

For what AChat collects and who receives it, see [Data, privacy & deletion](/concepts/data-privacy-and-deletion).
