# Personal chats & contacts

**Personal chats are for the people you've accepted as contacts, and they're end-to-end encrypted.** They need an account, because friends find you by your username.

## Add a contact

Open **Contacts** and add someone by:

- **Username**: search for it.
- **Invite link**: anyone with your link can send you a contact request.
- **QR code**: show your contact code, or scan theirs.

## Message requests

Messages from people who aren't your contacts wait in **Requests** until you choose.

- Someone you haven't accepted can send one message, and any media they send stays hidden until you accept.
- Accepting adds them to your contacts. By default, contacts can see your status and when you were last seen.
- You can report a request, choosing a reason.

## Block someone

A blocked person can't message you or send you requests, and isn't told. You can unblock them in Contacts.

## Online status and read receipts

| Setting | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Online status | Everyone, your contacts, or nobody | Contacts |
| Read receipts | On or off | On. Turn them off and you won't see when others read yours either |

## Chat list

- **Pin**, **Mark as unread** and **Mute notifications** from a chat's menu.
- An unread mention of you adds **@** beside the count.
- An unsent draft shows in the chat list.
- **Archive** a chat to tidy your list. Nothing changes for anyone else in it.

## Who can read a personal chat

The people in it. AChat's moderators see a message only if someone in the chat reports it. See [Passwords & encryption](/features/passwords-and-encryption).

## Starting something new

With no chat open, the main area offers **Message a person**, **New group**, **Create an anonymous room** and **Join a room** (paste a room link or ID), and your recent chats. The **+** above the chat list, the button on a phone, and Alt+N open the same choices.

## Adding a contact by invite link or QR code

**Add a contact** has three ways: **Username**, **Invite link** and **QR code**. Your invite link and your code carry `/invite/` and your username; anyone who opens or scans it sees you in **Add a contact** and can send you a contact request. **Scan a code** reads someone's code with your camera, where your browser can.

## Typing

In a personal chat or a group, a line above the message box shows when someone is typing. Nothing is stored, and anonymous rooms never show it. If your presence is set to **Nobody**, others don't see you typing.
